# Keynote I

## **Title:** </br>Building the ICT Infrastructure for the AI Inference Era: IOWN Initiatives and Ecosystems

## **Speaker:** </br>Dr. Yosuke Aragane</br>Vice President</br>Deputy Head of IOWN Integrated Innovation Center</br>NTT

## **Abstract:**

We are now entering into the era of AI inference, where anyone can use AI for their business and life. AI inference requires several key characteristics of ICT infrastructure that differ from AI training, such as end-to-end connectivity, data privacy/sovereignty/security, system resilience, ease of application deployment, and fast response. For ICT infrastructure, AI inference requires several important characteristics differ from AI training, such as end-to-end connectivity, data privacy/sovereignty/security, system resiliency, easy to deploy applications, and fast response. Comprehensive orchestration, between fixed and wireless, DC and DC, and platform and platform is essential to ensure these requirements. Involvement of multiple stakeholders and open communities is also important for interoperability, security, and application ecosystems. Engaging multiple stakeholders and open community is also important for inter-operability, security, and application ecosystem. IOWN (Innovative Optical and Wireless Network) is a photonics powered next generation ICT infrastructure for the era of AI with diverse players and communities. This talk will cover IOWN's use case efforts for diverse industries, distributed AI computing infrastructure, and open community ecosystems.
